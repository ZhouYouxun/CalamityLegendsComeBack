[h1]Calamity Legends Come Back [灾厄-传奇][/h1]

这是一个给 Calamity Mod 做“传奇武器回归与重制”的模组。

本模组会围绕每一把传奇武器进行重置、改造和可玩性开发。
重点不是把一个攻击模板复制几遍，再随着流程改伤害数字。
我们希望传奇武器的玩法会逐步推进，新的阶段真的带来新的操作、机制和战斗节奏。

当前 1.0 ~ 1.x 版本会专注于 SHPC 的 bug 修复。
SHPC 现在有材料装填、不同弹药效果、右键热量激光、配饰强化和大招超级激光。

模组里也会有一些重要的 QOL 内容。
比如部分让人血压上来的物品补配方、一些流程体验调整。
以及现在版本里的传奇补给包、复古游戏机包、高维矩阵核心、SHPP 平台、神镐、隔音耳罩和少量调试工具。

整体观感会尽量贴合灾厄模组。
特效、节奏和表现力会往灾厄本体的方向靠，目标是让东西看起来别太突兀
最好像是本来就该在这个世界里。

[h1]English Version[/h1]

Calamity Legends Come Back is an unofficial legendary-weapon rework mod for Calamity Mod.

This mod aims to rebuild, reshape, and expand the playability of every legendary weapon.
The important part is progression in gameplay, not copying one attack template and changing the damage number at each stage. A legendary weapon should gain new inputs, mechanics, and combat rhythm as it grows.

Version 1.0 ~ 1.x focuses on SHPC bug fixes.
SHPC currently has material loading, different ammo effects, right-click heat lasers, accessory upgrades, and a final super-laser ultimate.

The mod will also include some important QOL content.
For example, extra recipes for items that can be annoying to obtain, and progression comfort changes.
Current extras also include the Legendary Supply Box, retro game console pack, Hyperdimensional Matrix Core, SHPP platform, ShenGao pickaxe, Soundproof Earmuffs, and a few debug tools.

The overall presentation will try to stay close to Calamity Mod.
Effects, pacing, and visual style will lean toward Calamity itself. The goal is to keep new content from feeling too out of place,
ideally as if it belonged in this world from the start.
