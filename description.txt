[h1]Calamity Legends Come Back[/h1]

[h2]灾厄：传奇归来[/h2]

[quote]传奇不该在流程中被遗忘，也不该只是一串更大的数字。[/quote]

这是一个面向 Calamity Mod 的传奇武器重制模组。

我们将早期版本中淡出流程的传奇武器重新带回游戏，并赋予它们应有的身份：一把能够陪伴玩家走过漫长旅途、不断改变玩法的武器。

[code]Legendary Weapon = Progression + New Mechanics + New Ways to Fight[/code]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]不只是数值成长[/h2]

这不是把旧武器套进模板、再在每个阶段增加一组伤害数字。

[strike]单纯的数值堆叠[/strike] [b]机制与战斗方式的成长[/b]

随着游戏进度推进，传奇武器不仅会获得更高的伤害，也会逐步解锁新的攻击形态、技能机制与战斗逻辑。每次成长都应当带来新的选择、节奏或操作，而不只是让同一种攻击打得更痛。

[list]
[*][b]恢复传奇身份：[/b]重制并回归早期版本的传奇武器体验。
[*][b]非数值提升：[/b]流程推进会带来新的攻击方式与技能，而非只提升面板。
[*][b]持续的新鲜感：[/b]同一把武器在不同阶段拥有不同的战斗体验。
[*][b]全流程定位：[/b]目标是让传奇武器能够跨越多个阶段，而不是很快被替换。
[*][b]表现力重制：[/b]弹幕、特效、贴图与命中反馈都会围绕“传奇”重新塑造。
[/list]

[spoiler][i]有些成长不会直接写在伤害数字上，而会在你真正把武器拿起来使用时显现。[/i][/spoiler]

[h2]战斗方式[/h2]

不同传奇武器会拥有各自的机制与输入设计。根据武器特性，战斗可包含：

[olist]
[*]左键主攻击与连段
[*]右键独立技能或形态
[*]左、右键组合操作
[*]冲刺键与特殊技能键互动
[*]随流程解锁的攻击形态与资源循环
[/olist]

[h1]English Version[/h1]

[h2]Calamity Legends Come Back[/h2]

[quote]Legends should not be forgotten during progression, nor reduced to larger numbers.[/quote]

Calamity Legends Come Back is a legendary-weapon rework mod for Calamity Mod.

It brings back legendary weapons that faded from earlier versions and restores their intended identity: weapons that can accompany a player through a long journey while continually changing how they play.

[code]Legendary Weapon = Progression + New Mechanics + New Ways to Fight[/code]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]More Than Numerical Scaling[/h2]

This is not a template that merely increases a weapon's damage at each stage.

[strike]Numbers alone[/strike] [b]New mechanics and new combat expression[/b]

As progression advances, legendary weapons gain damage [i]and[/i] unlock new attack forms, abilities, and combat logic. Each upgrade is meant to create a new choice, rhythm, or input pattern instead of simply making the same attack hit harder.

[list]
[*][b]Legendary identity restored:[/b] Earlier legendary weapon experiences are rebuilt and returned.
[*][b]Non-numerical upgrades:[/b] Progress unlocks new attacks and abilities, not only better stats.
[*][b]Lasting freshness:[/b] The same weapon plays differently across stages of progression.
[*][b]Progression-spanning design:[/b] Legendary weapons are intended to remain meaningful across multiple stages.
[*][b]Presentation rebuilt:[/b] Projectiles, effects, textures, and combat feedback are remade around the idea of a legend.
[/list]

[spoiler][i]Some upgrades are not visible in the damage number. They reveal themselves when you put the weapon to use.[/i][/spoiler]

[h2]How You Fight[/h2]

Each legendary weapon has its own mechanics and input design. Depending on the weapon, combat can include:

[olist]
[*]Primary attacks and attack chains
[*]Independent right-click abilities or forms
[*]Left- and right-click combinations
[*]Dash-key and special-skill-key interactions
[*]Attack forms and resource loops unlocked by progression
[/olist]
